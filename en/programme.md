# Programme

Below you will find the programme for the 2026 Varjokirjamessut. The programme will take place over the weekend mainly at the Naapuruustalo Pasila, but on Saturday some events will also be held at Pasila Library. As usual, some of the programme will also be streamed on Varjokirjamessut’s [Youtube channel](https://youtube.com/@varjokirjamessut).

Programs in English are marked below with **ENG**.
