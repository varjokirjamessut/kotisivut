# Location


## Rauhanasema (Veturitori 3)

You can find the exhibitors and the vegan café on Saturday and Sunday from 10.30 am to 7.00 pm at Rauhanasema, Veturitori 3. This year, the programme will take place at Naapuruustalo Pasila (Fri, Sat, Sun) and Pasila Library (Sat).

## Naapuruustalo Pasila (Topparikuja 2)

This year, most of the programme will take place at the Naapuruustalo Pasila, Topparikuja 2. The first event begins on Friday at 6 pm. On Saturday and Sunday, events at the Neighbourhood Centre will run from 12 noon to 8 pm. Further details can be found from [Programme](/en/programme).

## Pasila Library (Kellosilta 9)

On Saturday, part of the programme will also take place at Pasila Library, at Kellosilta 9, from 10.00 to 16.00. The programme will be held in the auditorium on the second floor of the library. Pasila Library is located about 100 metres from Rauhanasema.
